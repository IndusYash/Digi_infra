# About Digi_infra

## Project Overview

This project is about digitalizing the whole of MMM infrastructure. The primary goal is to completely eliminate the traditional system of No Dues.

## Objective

By digitizing the entire MMM infrastructure, we aim to:

- **Eliminate Manual Processes**: Remove the paper-based No Dues system
- **Streamline Operations**: Automate the clearance and approval workflows
- **Improve Efficiency**: Reduce processing time and human errors
- **Enhance Transparency**: Provide real-time tracking and status updates
- **Digital Record Keeping**: Maintain comprehensive digital records for better accessibility and management

## Vision

To create a fully digital ecosystem for MMM infrastructure that removes the need for the traditional No Dues system, making processes faster, more transparent, and efficient for all stakeholders.
